01 · The Problem

A gap hiding
in plain sight.

In 2018, the Johannesburg market for healthy, preservative-free juice was almost nonexistent — particularly in communities outside the metropolitan core. Commercial juices were loaded with refined sugars. Fresh alternatives were either unavailable or inaccessible to people with disposable income who genuinely wanted healthier options.

"The fruit and vegetable juice category had not yet entered mainstream consciousness in South Africa. We were early — and in a market with real demand and real spending power."

The opportunity
No preservatives No refined sugars Fresh daily Underserved market Outside metro areas

The User
Health-conscious consumers outside Johannesburg's metropolitan core. People with disposable income who wanted better options but had no access to them at their local markets.

The Gap
No affordable, fresh, preservative-free juice was available at weekend markets in these communities. The category simply didn't exist at the accessible price point we could offer.

2018 was just before fruit and vegetable juice mixes became mainstream in South Africa. We entered an uncontested space before the competition arrived.
02 · The PM Process

Product thinking
without the title.

Every decision we made at The Juice Bar maps to a PM principle — without us knowing the frameworks at the time. This is the product story in PM language.

MVP Launch
Started with 4 flavours at one market. Small batch, low overhead, fast feedback.
We didn't over-invest before validating demand. The smallest possible version shipped first.
Behavioural Data
Kept a manual log of who visited the stall and who converted to buyers.
A hand-built analytics system. We tracked demographics and purchase behaviour to identify our ICP without any tools.
ICP Discovery
Identified our core user: women aged 19–55, health-conscious, motivated by fresh ingredients and no preservatives.
We didn't assume this — we discovered it through real market observation.
Activation Friction
Introduced free tasting samples in a shot glass. People didn't believe beetroot juice could taste good.
Removing the barrier to first experience converted sceptics into buyers. Exactly what Spotify does with the free tier.
Supply Optimisation
Reduced batch sizes when leftover stock was spoiling.
Matched production to real demand signals — not assumptions. Data-driven inventory management at a market stall.
Competitive Differentiation
Deprioritised orange juice — stronger competition, no differentiated advantage.
Doubled down on fruit and veg mixes where we had a clear edge and no direct competition. Portfolio rationalisation.
Strategic Partnership
Approached a large fruit and veg retailer to handle production at scale.
Removed an operational bottleneck through partnership — freeing us to focus on the customer experience at market.
03 · Product & Traction

Simple product.
Real results.

Final product range
🟣 Beetroot and Apple
Hero product. Highest conversion after sampling. Sceptics became loyal repeat buyers once they tasted it.
🟠 Carrot and Orange
Accessible entry point for first-time veg juice drinkers. Familiar flavour profile that built trust.
🟢 Spinach and Apple
Targeted at health-conscious early adopters. Our differentiated product for the segment willing to experiment.
🟡 Orange — deprioritised
Removed. Competition was stronger and we had no differentiated advantage. Resources redirected to winning SKUs.

04 · The Lesson

The only product
that found fit.

Of three products built, The Juice Bar was the only one where users came back. No app. No investment. No marketing budget. Just a real problem, a specific user, a product that delivered, and the discipline to iterate based on what the market was actually telling us.
